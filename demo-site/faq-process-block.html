Questions founders ask

Straight answers before you book a call.

How long does a first workflow take?

Most first workflows land in 2–3 weeks. Discovery call, fixed scope, build, deploy, handoff. Bigger suites are scoped per workflow.

Do I need to use n8n or Composio forever?

No. We build with what fits your stack: n8n, Composio, Make, Zapier-style logic, or direct API work. You own the automation after handover.

What if the automation breaks after launch?

Every project includes a support window (30 days standard, 60 days on the Automation Suite). We fix bugs and tweaks. After that, there are retainer options.

What access do you need from me?

Test tokens, a staging environment where possible, and 30–60 minutes with the person who knows the manual process best. We don't need full admin on day one.
